About Harborline

Harborline is a test publication — a fictional San Francisco city-life magazine built to exercise page structure and navigation for the TinySurveys WebSDK, including Content Navigation's Article detection (JSON-LD + og:type=article).

All stories on this site are original placeholder content written for testing and are not reporting on real people, businesses, or events.
