The shortest supply chain in the neighborhood runs about four hundred feet, straight down a stairwell.

On top of a parking structure that once just held cars, a two-year-old rooftop farm now grows the salad greens, herbs, and edible flowers for six restaurants within walking distance. There's no delivery truck involved — a runner wheels the harvest down twice a week, timed to land just before dinner prep.

## Small yield, short trip

The farm will never rival a real distributor on volume. What it offers instead is speed: greens picked that morning instead of trucked in from three counties over, and a growing list of chefs willing to build a menu around whatever's ready that week.
