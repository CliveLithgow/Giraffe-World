Ask a tailor in the Mission how business has changed and the answer is always the same: the clientele got younger, and it happened fast.

Five years ago, alteration shops mostly saw wedding parties and the occasional suit. Now they're hemming thrifted jeans, taking in blazers bought a size too big on purpose, and building relationships with clients who visit more often than they visit a hair salon.

## Buying less, fitting better

The shift tracks with a broader move away from disposable clothing. If you're only going to buy a handful of pieces a year, the logic goes, they should actually fit. That has turned a once-forgotten trade into one of the harder reservations to land in the city.

One shop owner put it simply: "We used to be where clothes went before a big day. Now we're where clothes go before every day."
