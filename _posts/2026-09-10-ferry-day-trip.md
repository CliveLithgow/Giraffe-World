Most day-trip advice starts with a rental car. This one starts with a ferry ticket you can buy on the dock ten minutes before departure.

The route was built for commuters, which is exactly why it works for a spontaneous trip: frequent sailings, no reservation, and a view of the skyline that most visitors only see from a tour boat that costs three times as much.

## Timing the crossing

Locals favor the mid-morning sailing, timed to land just as the harborside cafes open and well before the afternoon wind picks up. Bikes ride free in the cargo hold, which turns the far shore's flat coastal road into the rest of the itinerary without any extra planning required.
