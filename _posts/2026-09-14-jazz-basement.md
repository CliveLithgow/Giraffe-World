There's no marquee, no listing, and no name on the buzzer. You find out about the Wednesday set below the laundromat on Foss Street the way people have found out about it for six years: someone tells you.

Inside, the room holds maybe fifty people, folding chairs included. The lineup rotates through a loose circle of local players, and sets run long because nobody's paying by the hour.

## Word of mouth as the whole marketing plan

The organizers have turned down write-ups more than once, worried a bigger crowd would kill the thing that makes it work. For now, the door stays unmarked, the sets stay unannounced, and the fifty seats keep filling anyway.
