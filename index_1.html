Royal Farros, Managing Director

Royal Farros has spent over three decades as a technology entrepreneur and investor. Royal is founder and Managing Director of Farros Capital, providing funding, mentorship, and M&A/business advisory services to technology companies.

Royal is also founder and CEO of Big Heart Technology, reinventing both market research (TinySurveys™) and non-profit fundraising (Big Heart Trivia™) for a mobile world. He is also a strategic advisor to several companies, including Logic, Inc., creating the world's first physical AI robotic/transportation/data device operating system LogicOS.

Before Farros Capital, Royal was CEO and Chairman of portfolio company IMSI/Design, acquired by an investment group out of Mentor Graphics in 2016; co-founded and was CEO of MessageCast, Inc., acquired by Microsoft (NASD:MSFT) in 2005; founded and was CEO of iPrint.com (NASD: IPRT), taken public in 2000; was an Officer and General Manager at Deluxe Corporation (NYSE:DLX); and was Chairman and EVP of T/Maker Company (acquired by Deluxe Corporation).

A San Jose Business Journal Entrepreneur of the Year award winner and previously named one of Smart Computing's Top 25 Internet Executives, Royal has been widely interviewed and quoted, has appeared numerous times on CNBC and other financial and high tech venues, has guest lectured at both Stanford Graduate School of Business and Stanford Law School, and mentors and teaches at Draper University school for entrepreneurialism.
