Quick trivia

Fun facts, fast.

A shorter, snackable list — good for the one-minute version.

  • 01

    A newborn giraffe calf falls about two meters to the ground at birth — and is usually standing within an hour.

  • 02

    Giraffes are ruminants, like cows, and chew cud — you can often see the movement travel down their long neck.

  • 03

    A giraffe can run in short bursts up to roughly 55–60 km/h (35mph), despite its ungainly-looking gait.

  • 04

    There's ongoing scientific debate over whether giraffes are one species with several subspecies, or up to four distinct species — genetic studies have reopened the question in recent years.

  • 05

    Giraffes are mostly silent to human ears, but do communicate — including infrasound calls too low-pitched for people to hear.

  • 06

    Male giraffes fight by swinging their necks and heads at each other in a behavior called "necking," which can double as a mating display or a real contest for dominance.

  • 07

    A giraffe's eyes are among the largest of any land mammal, giving it excellent wide-angle vision to spot predators across open terrain.

  • 08

    Gestation lasts about 15 months — one of the longest of any land mammal.
